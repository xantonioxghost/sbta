Build a mobile app called "Posture Monitor" that connects to an ESP32-based wearable device over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to track and alert on posture in real time.

Core functionality:

BLE Connection
Scan for and connect to a BLE device advertising as "PostureBelt" (or a custom service UUID I'll provide)
Show connection status clearly (Connected / Searching / Disconnected)
Auto-reconnect if the connection drops
Live Posture Dashboard
Display the current tilt/deviation angle received from the device in real time (updated ~5x per second)
Simple visual indicator: green when posture is good (within threshold), red/orange when deviation exceeds the alert angle
A simple animated icon or silhouette that tilts to visually represent current posture
Calibration
A "Calibrate" button that sends a command to the device telling it to reset its baseline (user sits/stands straight and taps this)
Show a countdown or "hold still" message during calibration
Alerts
Push notification or in-app alert when bad posture is sustained for more than X seconds (configurable)
Adjustable sensitivity threshold (slider from ~10° to ~30°)
Option to toggle vibration/sound alert on the phone itself, separate from the device's own buzzer/LED
History & Stats
Log posture sessions locally (on-device storage, no cloud needed for v1)
Simple daily summary: % of time in good posture vs. bad posture
A basic line/bar chart of posture deviation over the current session
Settings
Alert threshold angle
Alert delay (how many seconds of bad posture before alerting)
Enable/disable notifications
Device connection management (forget device, reconnect)

Technical constraints:

Target platform: [specify — iOS / Android / cross-platform React Native or Flutter]
BLE communication: expect the ESP32 to send simple text or JSON strings like {"angle": 12.4, "alert": false} over a BLE characteristic — parse this on each update
Calibration command sent as a simple string like "CALIBRATE" written to a BLE characteristic
No backend/server required for v1 — everything runs locally on the phone
Keep the UI simple and readable at a glance (this will be checked occasionally throughout the day, not stared at continuously)

Design tone: clean, minimal, health/wellness app aesthetic — not clinical or intimidating.
